Platform Questions

Are the courses completely online?

Yes. All lessons, projects, and assessments are 100% online. You can learn from anywhere at your own pace.

How do I create a free student account?

Simple: click on any "Start Course" button on our catalog and input your name and email to initialize your lessons.

Academics

Certifications & Quizzes

Learn about grading criteria and standard certification documentation.

Do I get a certificate after completion?

Absolutely! Once you complete all module videos and score above 70% in the coursework quizzes, you will receive a digital certificate.

Can I retake final course assessments if I fail?

Yes, you can retake the assessments after a cool-down period of 24 hours. Tutors recommend re-watching previous video exercises before retaking.
